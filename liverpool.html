DISCOVER LIVERPOOL

A City of Music & Maritime Heritage

Liverpool is a lively waterfront city with a rich cultural identity, famous football clubs and a remarkable maritime past.

Best known around the world for its connection to The Beatles, Liverpool has also become a vibrant destination for museums, galleries, restaurants, shopping and nightlife.

From the historic Royal Albert Dock to colourful neighbourhoods and iconic music landmarks, Liverpool offers plenty to discover on a city break.

GETTING AROUND

Explore Liverpool Your Way

Liverpool's compact city centre makes many of its best-known attractions easy to reach on foot, while buses and trains connect you with neighbourhoods further away.

The waterfront is especially enjoyable to explore on foot, allowing you to combine sightseeing with views across the River Mersey.

Explore Like a Local

Walk between central attractions whenever possible and use public transport for places outside the city centre.

Merseyrail

A useful rail network for travelling around Liverpool and nearby areas.

City Bus

Regular bus services connect the city centre with surrounding neighbourhoods and attractions.

Walking

Explore the waterfront, historic centre and many major attractions comfortably on foot.

Taxi

Taxis can be useful when travelling with luggage or reaching destinations outside the central area.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit Liverpool

Liverpool can be enjoyed throughout the year, with every season bringing a different atmosphere to the city.

MAR — MAY

Spring

Enjoy milder weather, fresh greenery and comfortable conditions for exploring the waterfront and city centre.

JUN — AUG

Summer

Longer daylight hours and a lively atmosphere make summer a great time for waterfront walks and outdoor events.

MOST POPULAR
SEP — NOV

Autumn

Cooler days and quieter periods create a relaxed atmosphere for museums, galleries and city walks.

DEC — FEB

Winter

Enjoy festive events, cosy cafés, museums and Liverpool's lively indoor attractions.

Liverpool Travel Tips

A few practical tips to help you enjoy Liverpool comfortably and make the most of your visit.

01

Explore the Waterfront on Foot

The Royal Albert Dock and nearby attractions are easy to explore on foot, making walking a great way to enjoy the waterfront.

02

Pack for Changeable Weather

Liverpool weather can change quickly, so bring comfortable layers and a lightweight waterproof jacket.

03

Explore the Music Heritage

Leave time to discover Liverpool's famous music history, from The Beatles Story to legendary music venues.

04

06

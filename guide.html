Plan Around the Seasons

The Best Time to Visit Britain

Every season offers a different way to experience the United Kingdom. Choose the time that best matches your travel style.

March – May

Spring

Enjoy colourful gardens, blooming countryside and pleasant days as Britain welcomes spring.

Great for gardens & sightseeing
June – August

Summer

Experience longer days, outdoor attractions, festivals and vibrant city life across Britain.

Great for outdoor adventures
September – November

Autumn

Discover golden landscapes, quieter attractions and beautiful autumn colours across the UK.

Great for scenery & culture
December – February

Winter

Enjoy festive markets, cosy experiences, historic cities and Britain's magical winter atmosphere.

Great for Christmas & city breaks

Before You Travel

UK Visa & Entry Guide

Make sure you understand the entry requirements before travelling to Britain. Your requirements depend on your nationality, travel purpose and length of stay.

Check Your Visa

Find out whether you need a UK visa before planning your journey.

Check ETA

Some visitors may need an Electronic Travel Authorisation before travelling.

Prepare Documents

Keep your passport and required supporting documents ready.

Apply Early

Allow enough time to complete your application before your planned trip.

Check Official Requirements
Always check the latest requirements on the official UK Government website before travelling.

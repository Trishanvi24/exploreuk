DISCOVER YORK

A City of History & Character

York is one of England's most fascinating historic cities, surrounded by ancient walls and filled with centuries of stories, remarkable architecture and charming medieval streets.

Wander through the famous Shambles, admire the magnificent York Minster and explore the city's fascinating museums and historic landmarks.

From peaceful riverside walks and traditional pubs to independent shops and cultural attractions, York offers a memorable experience for every traveller.

GETTING AROUND

Explore York Your Way

York's historic centre is compact and easy to explore on foot. Many of the city's best-known attractions, shops and historic streets are close together.

Local buses are useful for travelling beyond the city centre, while York railway station provides convenient connections to other UK cities.

Explore Like a Local

Give yourself time to walk around the historic centre. York's small lanes and hidden corners are often best discovered on foot.

Walking

The historic centre is compact, making walking one of the easiest ways to explore York's main attractions.

Local Bus

Bus services connect the city centre with surrounding areas and attractions outside the historic core.

Train

York railway station offers excellent connections to destinations across England and the wider UK.

Taxi

Taxis are a convenient option for short journeys or reaching locations outside the central walking area.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit York

York is a year-round destination, with every season offering a different atmosphere and experience.

MAR — MAY

Spring

Enjoy fresh greenery, blooming gardens and pleasant conditions for exploring York's historic streets, walls and outdoor attractions.

JUN — AUG

Summer

Longer daylight hours make summer ideal for walking the city walls, sightseeing and enjoying York's riverside areas.

MOST POPULAR
SEP — NOV

Autumn

Cooler days and colourful autumn scenery create a relaxed setting for historic walks, museums and cosy cafés.

DEC — FEB

Winter

Experience a quieter side of York with historic attractions, museums, cosy pubs and a charming winter atmosphere.

TRAVEL SMART

York Travel Tips

A few practical tips to help you enjoy York's historic streets, landmarks, museums and local experiences.

01

Wear Comfortable Shoes

York is ideal for exploring on foot, so comfortable shoes are essential for walking through historic streets and along the city walls.

02

Start Your Day Early

Begin your sightseeing early to enjoy popular attractions and the historic centre before the busiest periods.

03

Check the Weather

UK weather can change quickly, so layers and a lightweight waterproof jacket are useful when exploring York outdoors.

04

Check Attraction Times

Check current opening times and visitor information for major attractions before you travel, especially during busy seasons.

05

Explore Beyond the Main Streets

Give yourself time to discover quieter lanes, riverside paths, independent shops and less crowded corners of the city.

06

Plan Your Route

Group nearby attractions together to make the most of your time and reduce unnecessary walking between different parts of the city.

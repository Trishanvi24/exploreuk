DISCOVER MANCHESTER

A City of Culture & Character

Manchester is one of the UK's most exciting cities, blending a fascinating industrial past with a modern creative spirit.

From legendary football and world-famous music to impressive architecture, independent cafés and vibrant neighbourhoods, Manchester offers something for every kind of traveller.

Spend your days exploring museums and historic streets, watching a football match, discovering the city's food scene or simply enjoying its energetic atmosphere.

GETTING AROUND

Explore Manchester Your Way

Manchester has an extensive public transport network, making it easy to travel between the city centre, neighbourhoods and major attractions.

The city centre is also compact enough to explore many popular sights on foot.

Explore Like a Local

Combine walking with public transport to explore more of Manchester without spending unnecessary time travelling between attractions.

Metrolink Tram

A convenient way to travel across Manchester and reach many areas beyond the city centre.

City Bus

Regular bus services connect the city centre with surrounding neighbourhoods and attractions.

Train

Manchester's railway stations provide useful connections to cities and towns across the UK.

Walking

Many central attractions are close together, making walking an enjoyable way to discover the city.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit Manchester

Manchester is a year-round destination, with each season offering a different way to experience the city.

MAR — MAY

Spring

Enjoy milder days, fresh greenery and a comfortable atmosphere for exploring museums, neighbourhoods and outdoor spaces.

JUN — AUG

Summer

Longer days, outdoor events and a lively city atmosphere make summer a popular time to visit.

MOST POPULAR
SEP — NOV

Autumn

Cooler temperatures and colourful parks create a great setting for museums, cafés and city walks.

DEC — FEB

Winter

Enjoy festive events, cosy restaurants and the city's indoor attractions during the colder months.

TRAVEL SMART

Manchester Travel Tips

A few useful tips to help you explore Manchester comfortably and make the most of your visit.

01

Explore the City Centre on Foot

Many central attractions are close together, so walking is a great way to discover Manchester's streets and neighbourhoods.

02

Pack for Changing Weather

Manchester weather can be unpredictable. Bring layers and a lightweight waterproof jacket for comfortable sightseeing.

03

Plan Football Visits Early

If football is part of your trip, check stadium tour and match information ahead of time, especially during busy periods.

04

Check the Event Calendar

Manchester has a busy programme of concerts, exhibitions and cultural events, so check what's happening during your visit.

05

Use Public Transport

Trams, buses and trains make it easy to reach attractions outside the central area without relying on a car.

06

Explore Beyond the Centre

Leave time to discover creative neighbourhoods, canals, independent cafés and hidden corners away from the main tourist routes.

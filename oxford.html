DISCOVER OXFORD

A City of Dreaming Spires

Oxford is one of England's most historic and beautiful cities, best known for its prestigious university, magnificent architecture and centuries of academic tradition.

Wander through atmospheric medieval streets, explore historic college courtyards and enjoy the peaceful waterways that have shaped the character of the city.

Beyond its famous university, Oxford offers fascinating museums, independent bookshops, traditional pubs, charming cafés and beautiful green spaces.

GETTING AROUND

Explore Oxford Your Way

Oxford's historic centre is compact and many of its most famous sights are within easy walking distance. Exploring on foot is one of the best ways to appreciate the city's architecture and atmosphere.

Buses provide convenient connections around Oxford, while trains are useful for travelling to and from the city and exploring nearby destinations.

Explore Like a Local

Wear comfortable shoes and explore the historic centre on foot whenever possible. Use buses for areas further from the centre.

City Bus

Regular bus services connect Oxford city centre with surrounding areas and attractions.

Train

Oxford railway station provides useful connections to London and other UK destinations.

Walking

Walking is ideal for exploring historic colleges, museums, streets and central landmarks.

Taxi

Taxis can be useful for luggage, late travel or destinations outside the central area.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit Oxford

Oxford is beautiful throughout the year, with each season offering a different way to experience its historic streets, colleges and green spaces.

MAR — MAY

Spring

Enjoy fresh greenery, blooming gardens and comfortable conditions for walking around the historic city centre.

JUN — AUG

Summer

Longer daylight hours make summer ideal for exploring colleges, enjoying riverside walks and going punting.

MOST POPULAR
SEP — NOV

Autumn

Golden foliage and cooler temperatures create a beautiful atmosphere for museums, walking tours and historic sights.

DEC — FEB

Winter

Enjoy quieter streets, cosy cafés, museums and the atmospheric charm of Oxford during the colder months.

TRAVEL SMART

Oxford Travel Tips

A few practical tips to help you explore Oxford comfortably and make the most of your visit.

01

Explore on Foot

Oxford's historic centre is compact, so many famous colleges, museums and landmarks are easy to explore on foot.

02

Pack for Changeable Weather

Bring comfortable layers and a lightweight waterproof jacket, as the weather can change during the day.

03

Check College Opening Times

Some colleges have restricted visiting hours or may close for university events, so check before visiting.

04

Try Punting

A punting trip is a relaxing way to experience Oxford and enjoy beautiful views along the River Cherwell.

05

Use Public Transport

Walking is best for the centre, while buses are useful for reaching areas and attractions further away.

06

Explore Beyond the Main Streets

Wander into quieter lanes and discover independent bookshops, cafés, pubs and beautiful hidden corners.

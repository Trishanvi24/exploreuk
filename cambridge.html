DISCOVER CAMBRIDGE

A City of Historic Colleges

Cambridge is one of England's most beautiful university cities, celebrated for its historic colleges, elegant architecture and centuries of academic tradition.

Wander through atmospheric streets, explore magnificent college courtyards and enjoy peaceful walks along the River Cam.

From world-class museums and historic libraries to traditional pubs, independent bookshops and riverside cafés, Cambridge offers plenty to discover.

GETTING AROUND

Explore Cambridge Your Way

Cambridge city centre is compact and easy to explore. Walking and cycling are two of the best ways to discover the historic colleges, museums and beautiful streets.

Local buses connect the city centre with surrounding neighbourhoods, while trains provide convenient connections to London and other destinations.

Explore Like a Local

Wear comfortable shoes or consider cycling around Cambridge. The historic centre is best experienced at a relaxed pace.

Walking

Many famous colleges, museums and landmarks are within easy walking distance.

Cycling

Cycling is a popular way to travel around Cambridge and reach areas beyond the centre.

City Bus

Regular bus services connect the city centre with surrounding areas and attractions.

Train

Cambridge railway station provides convenient connections to London and other UK destinations.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit Cambridge

Cambridge is worth visiting throughout the year, with every season bringing its own atmosphere, colours and experiences.

MAR — MAY

Spring

Enjoy colourful gardens, fresh greenery and comfortable conditions for walking, cycling and exploring the historic colleges.

JUN — AUG

Summer

Longer days make summer ideal for punting, riverside walks, outdoor cafés and exploring Cambridge's beautiful college grounds.

MOST POPULAR
SEP — NOV

Autumn

Golden foliage and cooler weather create a peaceful atmosphere for walking tours, museums and historic sightseeing.

DEC — FEB

Winter

Enjoy quieter streets, cosy cafés, museums and the atmospheric charm of Cambridge during the colder months.

TRAVEL SMART

Cambridge Travel Tips

A few practical tips to help you explore Cambridge comfortably and enjoy more of the city.

01

Explore the Centre on Foot

Many historic colleges, museums, bridges and landmarks are close together, making walking a great way to explore.

02

Consider Cycling

Cycling is part of everyday life in Cambridge and can be a convenient way to explore areas beyond the historic centre.

03

Check College Access

Individual colleges may have different visitor arrangements and opening times, so check before planning your visit.

04

Book Punting Ahead

Punting is especially popular during busy periods, so booking ahead can help you secure your preferred time.

05

Prepare for UK Weather

Bring comfortable shoes and light waterproof layers so you can enjoy Cambridge even when the weather changes during the day.

06

Explore Beyond the Colleges

Leave time for museums, gardens, independent shops, cafés and quieter riverside areas away from the busiest landmarks.
